What's in the index

The lifestyle

A typical existing home (the median home is about 3 bed / 2 bath) bought with a 30-year mortgage, two new cars driven 15,000 miles a year, two children in full-time centre care, groceries and meals out, a 60" TV and a full set of kitchen and laundry appliances, two smartphones, home internet and cable, employer health coverage, a domestic family trip every year and an international one every five years, and saving enough to retire at 55–60.

How costs are dated

House prices follow the FHFA repeat-sales index and Freddie Mac mortgage rates. Health premiums are KFF's employer survey. Other items start from a 2024 national average (AAA, Child Care Aware, BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey) and move with the matching CPI. Phones, TVs, appliances, clothing and entertainment follow what married couples with children actually spent each year, because quality-adjusted prices for electronics imply a 2024 TV would have cost $50,000 in 1980.

Taxes and retirement

The family pays federal and state income tax and FICA, computed with NBER TAXSIM for every state and weighted by population; mortgage interest and property tax are itemized when that beats the standard deduction. Retirement saving funds spending from the retirement age through the longer-lived spouse's life expectancy plus three years, net of Social Security claimed at 62 and expected pension income, including private health insurance until Medicare.

Income benchmarks

Census CPS median household and married-couple family income (current dollars), and BLS median usual weekly earnings of full-time workers × 52. The interactive version recomputes housing, childcare and retirement in your browser and re-solves taxes on each year's TAXSIM schedule, holding itemized deductions at baseline.

Sources: FHFA House Price Index; Freddie Mac PMMS; BLS CPI-U and Consumer Expenditure Survey; Census CPS ASEC historical income tables; BLS weekly earnings; KFF Employer Health Benefits Survey; AAA Your Driving Costs; Child Care Aware of America; NBER TAXSIM 35; SSA and CDC life tables.
